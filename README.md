# Auloris

**Where Learning Meets Possibility.**

Auloris is a modern educational platform designed to make learning simpler, more accessible, and engaging for students, teachers, and educational organizations.

The platform brings together interactive quizzes, educational workbooks, and dedicated dashboards in one convenient digital learning environment.

## About Auloris

Auloris aims to support modern education by providing students with opportunities to practice their knowledge and teachers with resources to enhance classroom instruction.

With a clean, intuitive interface, Auloris makes educational tools easy to access and use.

## Our Features

### Student Dashboard

A personalized learning environment where students can complete interactive quizzes, review their answers, track their scores, and monitor their learning progress.

### Teacher Dashboard

A dedicated resource center where educators can browse, search, and download educational workbooks organized by subject and learning level.

### Staff Dashboard

A combined workspace providing staff members with access to educational resources, interactive quizzes, performance statistics, and workbook collections.

### Interactive Quizzes

Auloris offers quizzes covering Computers, Coding, and Robotics. Students and staff can test their understanding, receive immediate results, and review their answers.

### Educational Workbooks

A collection of downloadable PDF workbooks designed to support classroom instruction, independent learning, and educational practice.

## Our Educational Focus

Auloris focuses on three key areas of technology education:

- **Computers:** Developing digital literacy and understanding computer hardware, software, and technology.
- **Coding:** Building programming knowledge, logical reasoning, and computational thinking.
- **Robotics:** Exploring engineering principles, robotics systems, sensors, and programming.

## Our Mission

To make quality educational resources and modern learning tools accessible through a simple, engaging, and reliable digital platform.

## Our Vision

To empower the next generation of learners with the knowledge, confidence, and technological skills needed to succeed in an increasingly digital world.
